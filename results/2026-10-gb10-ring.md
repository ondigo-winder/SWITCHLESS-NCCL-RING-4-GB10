# Results: 4 × Dell Pro Max GB10 switchless ring, October 2026

## Setup

- 4 × Dell Pro Max GB10, DGX OS 7.6.0, kernel 7.0.0-1019-nvidia, driver 580.178.04
- ring: node1 –f0– node2 –f1– node3 –f0– node4 –f1– node1, one QSFP cable per edge,
  both PCIe halves addressed, MTU 9000
- bootstrap over a 10 GbE management LAN (`enP7s7`)
- container `nvcr.io/nvidia/pytorch:26.09-py3` used only as a CUDA 13 runtime;
  the benchmark is `tests/allreduce.cu` linked straight against the library under test
- bus bandwidth = bytes × 2(n−1)/n / time, float32 sum, 1 GPU per node

## Raw RDMA reference

`ib_write_bw` (perftest), RoCEv2, GID 3, MTU 4096:

## Soak

4 nodes, 4 HCAs, 8 channels, 9,000 timed all-reduces per size (just over 11 minutes):

- 1 GiB: 192.4 Gb/s, result correct, no NCCL warnings
- RoCE counters (`out_of_sequence`, `packet_seq_err`, `local_ack_timeout_err`,
  `rnr_nak_retry_err`, `implied_nak_seq_err`, `duplicate_request`) and PHY counters
  (`rx_crc_errors_phy`, `rx_symbol_err_phy`, `rx_discards_phy`, `rx_out_of_buffer`,
  `tx_discards_phy`): 0 before and after, on all 4 nodes and all 4 devices
- during the run node1 transmitted about 97 Gb/s on each half of its f0 cable

## After a reboot

All 4 nodes rebooted, nothing re-applied by hand (addresses and MTU are persistent
NetworkManager profiles). Four minutes after boot: all 16 ring interfaces up at
MTU 9000 with their addresses, all 16 RoCE devices ACTIVE with a GID at index 3.
Same 4-node, 4-HCA, 8-channel run:

| 1 MiB | 64 MiB | 1 GiB | Check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 39.3 Gb/s | 185.1 Gb/s | 193.1 Gb/s | 4 ✓ |
